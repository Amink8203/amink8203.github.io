Research
interests

I’m interested in how language models reason, how we evaluate their outputs, and how training incentives shape their reliability.

    Language understanding and retrieval-augmented generation for complex information tasks.

Automatic Design of MLP Networks for Classification Tasks Using Geometric Operators

Designed three deterministic methods that construct network architectures and weights directly from data geometry: Boundary Pairing, Chained PCA, and Supervised Refinement.
